> **In short:**
> 1. Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace are compared using their pricing pages checked on 22 November 2026, for businesses of up to 300 users.
> 2. Google Workspace Business Standard costs €13.60 per user per month with an annual commitment, with 2 TB of storage per person, compared with €20.36 excl. VAT for Microsoft 365 Business Standard and Copilot Business, which includes desktop apps.
> 3. Microsoft 365 Business Basic is the cheapest entry plan, at €6.07 excl. VAT per user per month billed annually, with 1 TB of storage but no desktop apps.
> 4. Google Workspace also offers no-commitment billing (€8.10 for Business Starter); its displayed prices are introductory prices for new customers, valid for 12 months.

## Why choose between Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace

Choosing between **Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace** means choosing the environment in which the team will write, calculate, communicate and store files for years. Switching later is possible, but time-consuming.

Both suites share the same basics: business email in the company's name, word processing, spreadsheets, presentations, cloud storage, calendar and video calls. Setting up an address in the company's name is covered in the guide to [creating a professional email address]({{< relref "create-professional-email-address.md" >}}).

Their difference lies mainly in philosophy: Google Workspace was designed for the browser and real-time collaboration, while Microsoft 365 extends the Office software installed on computers.

### The criteria used for the comparison

- **The price per user**, annual and without commitment.
- **Included storage** per person.
- **Apps**: online tools or desktop software.
- **Video calls and team chat.**
- **Security** offered in each plan.

## Google Workspace: the most economical online suite

Google Workspace brings together Gmail, Calendar, Meet, Chat, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides. According to its pricing page, checked on 22 November 2026, three plans target businesses of up to 300 users. Details are on [Google Workspace's pricing page](https://workspace.google.com/pricing).

| Google Workspace plan | Annual commitment | No commitment | Storage per person | Meet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business Starter | €6.80 | €8.10 | 30 GB | 100 participants |
| Business Standard | €13.60 | €16.20 | 2 TB | 150 participants, recording |
| Business Plus | €21.10 | €25.30 | 5 TB | 500 participants |

These prices, per user per month, are presented as introductory prices for new customers, applied to the first 20 users for 12 months. The standard rate that follows is not shown. A 50% discount for 3 months was also offered at the time of the check.

### Google Workspace's key features

- **Browser-based collaboration**: several people edit the same document at once, with no software to install.
- **Generous storage** from Business Standard: 2 TB per person, compared with 1 TB at Microsoft.
- **Chat team messaging** included in every plan.
- **Gemini AI assistant** in Gmail from Starter, then in Docs, Meet and other apps from Standard.
- **Business Standard** adds meeting recording, appointment booking pages and e-signature in Docs and PDFs.
- **No-commitment billing** available, and a 14-day free trial.

For a small business that works mainly online and shares many documents, Google Workspace offers the best balance of price, storage and simplicity.

## Microsoft 365: the suite for working with Office

Microsoft 365 brings together Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneDrive and Teams. Its pricing page for small and medium-sized businesses, checked on 22 November 2026, shows three plans billed annually with automatic renewal, for up to 300 users.

| Microsoft 365 plan | Price excl. VAT per user per month | What it brings |
|---|---|---|
| Business Basic | €6.07 | Web and mobile versions of Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook, email, Teams, 1 TB of storage |
| Business Standard and Copilot Business | €20.36 | Desktop apps, Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams |
| Business Premium and Copilot Business | €27.73 | Device security and management, phishing protection, sensitive data protection |

According to the Teams plan comparison page consulted on 9 November 2026, Business Standard allows the apps to be installed on 5 PCs or Macs, 5 tablets and 5 mobile devices per user, and Business Basic and Standard include 1 TB of cloud storage per user and a 100 GB mailbox.

### Microsoft 365's strengths

- **Desktop apps** Word, Excel and PowerPoint from Business Standard, essential for some jobs and complex files.
- **Teams** for team chat and meetings of up to 300 participants.
- **Cheapest entry plan** with Business Basic, at €6.07 excl. VAT.
- **Advanced security** in Business Premium, including device management and phishing protection.
- **Point to watch**: Microsoft lists plans "with Teams" and "without Teams", to be checked when subscribing.

Billed annually, according to the checks made on 22 November 2026:

| Tier | Google Workspace (10 users) | Microsoft 365 (10 users) |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | €68 per month (Business Starter) | €60.70 excl. VAT per month (Business Basic) |

| Advanced | €211 per month (Business Plus) | €277.30 excl. VAT per month (Business Premium and Copilot Business) |

Google prices are introductory and do not state VAT; Microsoft prices exclude VAT. The comparison gives an order of magnitude, not a quote.

## Who is each suite for? Recommendations by profile

### Small business that works mainly online

Google Workspace Business Standard is the most balanced choice: 2 TB per person, meeting recording, Gemini in the apps and a lower price than Microsoft's equivalent plan.

### Business that relies on desktop Excel or Word

Microsoft 365 Business Standard remains essential for teams handling complex Excel files or heavily formatted Word documents, or working with partners who use Office.

### Small team on a tight budget

Microsoft 365 Business Basic offers the lowest entry price, with 1 TB of storage. Google Workspace Business Starter costs slightly more, but offers no-commitment billing.

## How to choose your suite in 5 steps

1. **List the files and software in use**: reliance on Excel, Word templates or business tools.
2. **Count users** and calculate the annual cost, separating introductory and standard prices.
3. **Estimate the storage needed** per person.
4. **Plan security**: protection of accounts, devices and against phishing.
5. **Test with a small team** during the free trial.

Device security still needs to be planned, whatever the suite: the comparison of the [best antivirus for a small business]({{< relref "best-antivirus-small-business.md" >}}) covers Microsoft Defender for Business among others. The main families of collaboration tools are described in the article [Collaboration tools: the categories to know]({{< relref "team-collaboration-tools.md" >}}).

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Comparing an introductory price with a standard price.
2. Forgetting to check whether the chosen Microsoft plan includes Teams.
3. Underestimating the storage the team needs.
4. Migrating all mailboxes at once without a prior test.
5. Choosing without asking teams which software they actually use.
