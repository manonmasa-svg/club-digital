## Why a small business needs HR software

Choosing the **best HR software for a small business** means replacing leave spreadsheets, paper files and email exchanges with a single tool used by employees, managers and the person in charge of HR.

HR software, or an HRIS, does not calculate payroll. It manages employee data, absences, time, reviews and training. The difference between the two families of tools is detailed in the article [Payroll software: the essential features]({{< relref "payroll-software-key-features.md" >}}).

The two complement each other: absences recorded in the HR software feed payroll variables, then the French DSN filing. How that filing works is explained in the guide to the [DSN for a small business in France]({{< relref "dsn-small-business-france.md" >}}).

### The criteria used for the comparison

- **The pricing model**: per module, per user, per pack or tailored.
- **Transparency** of published prices.
- **The commitment** required.
- **Available modules**: leave, time, reviews, training, expense reports.
- **The link with payroll.**

Lucca offers around fifteen HR and administrative solutions, grouped into families: employee records, time and activities, talent, compensation and benefits, business expenses. Details are on [Lucca's pricing page](https://www.lucca.fr/tarifs/) (in French).

### Lucca's key features

- **Public prices per module**: the business only pays for the tools it uses.
- **No minimum commitment**, with a monthly count of users and a decreasing unit cost.
- **Dedicated expert consultant** for rollout and onboarding support.
- **Services included at no extra cost**: single sign-on (SSO), calendar sync, shared documents, dashboards.
- **Payroll link**: connectors to Silae and Yeap, online payslip distribution at €0.70 excl. VAT per document.
- **Service**: daily data backups, support on working days from 9 am to 5 pm.

For a small business that wants to start with leave and employee records, then add modules over time, Lucca offers the clearest pricing of the four.

## Comparative analysis: Eurécia, Factorial and PayFit against Lucca

### Eurécia, an entry price in packs of 10

Eurécia is French HR software offering an HR portal and modules: leave and absences, time and activities, scheduling, reviews, objectives, skills, training and expense reports. Its pricing page, checked on 23 November 2026, shows a price from €4.20 excl. VAT per employee per month, sold in packs of 10 users, i.e. €42 excl. VAT per month for the HR portal.

The price depends on the number of employees and the modules activated. Eurécia offers a 30-day free trial with no commitment and a dedicated expert consultant for rollout. Subscription commitment terms are set out in the contract.

### Factorial, the tailored all-in-one platform

Factorial combines employee data management, onboarding, e-signatures, leave, time tracking, scheduling, reviews, recruitment, expense reports and project management. Its pricing page shows a price "from €2.9 per user per month".

The plan is built on a tailored basis from the modules chosen, during a demo. The final price therefore depends on the combination selected, with no detailed price list published.

### PayFit, payroll and HR in the same tool

PayFit is first and foremost a payroll service, compared in the article on the [best payroll software in France]({{< relref "best-payroll-software-france.md" >}}). Its RH+ plan, checked on 11 November 2026, costs €30 per employee per month, plus a subscription of €49 excl. VAT per month for 1 to 3 employees, i.e. €139 per month for 3 employees, with no commitment.

RH+ adds review management, objectives, one-to-one meetings, 360-degree feedback and HR surveys to payroll. It is the most expensive option per employee, but the only one that includes payroll production.

## Who is each tool for? Recommendations by profile

### Small business that first wants to manage leave and employee records

Lucca lets you start with core HR and absences, then add performance, training or business expenses with no commitment, with a known price for each module.

### Small team that wants a simple budget

Eurécia shows an entry price per pack of 10 users and a 30-day free trial, handy for testing before signing.

### Business without a payroll manager

PayFit combines payroll production and HR features. The cost per employee is higher, but it replaces two tools and part of the administrative work.

## How to choose HR software in 5 steps

1. **List the processes to equip**: leave, time, reviews, training, expense reports.
2. **Choose a starting point**: often employee records and absences.
3. **Calculate the annual cost** based on headcount and modules.
4. **Check the link with payroll** and the accountant.
5. **Test with a pilot team** during the trial or after a demo.

HR software holds sensitive personal data: addresses, salaries, sick leave. [Small business cybersecurity best practices]({{< relref "small-business-cybersecurity-best-practices.md" >}}) apply to every login, starting with two-factor authentication.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Buying every module at once before rolling out the first ones.
2. Comparing a starting price with a per-module price.
3. Forgetting the link with payroll software and the DSN filing.
4. Neglecting training for managers, who approve leave and run reviews.
5. Ignoring the commitment terms in the contract.
