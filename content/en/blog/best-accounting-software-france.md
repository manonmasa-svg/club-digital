> 1. Four accounting software tools available in France are compared based on their pricing pages checked on 27 October 2026: Indy, Pennylane, Sage 50 and Tiime.
> 2. Indy is the only one of the four to advertise a plan in which a company prepares its own balance sheet, income statement and tax filings, at €49 excl. VAT per month on an annual commitment.
> 3. Sage 50 Accounting starts at €21 excl. VAT per month for up to 3 users, Pennylane charges €79 excl. VAT per month for its accounting module in the Premium plan for freelancers, and Tiime focuses on collaboration with a chartered accountant.
> 4. All four vendors appear on the official list of approved e-invoicing platforms published by the French tax authority (DGFiP).

## Why choosing accounting software matters in 2026

Choosing the **best accounting software** first depends on a simple question: who keeps the books? The business owner, an employee, or a chartered accountant working from the company's data? The four tools compared answer different situations.

E-invoicing is also changing the picture in France. According to [economie.gouv.fr](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/tout-savoir-sur-la-facturation-electronique-pour-les-entreprises) (page in French, consulted on 27 October 2026), all businesses must be able to receive electronic invoices since 1 September 2026, and small and micro businesses will have to issue them from 1 September 2027.

These invoices go through platforms approved by the State. The DGFiP publishes the [list of approved platforms](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/je-consulte-la-liste-des-plateformes-agreees) on impots.gouv.fr. In the version consulted on 27 October 2026 (page updated on 13 October 2026), Indy, Pennylane, Sage and Tiime all appear on the list of operators that have met all conditions, interoperability tests included.

### The criteria used for the comparison

- **Accounting scope**: data entry and categorisation, VAT returns, balance sheet, tax return.
- **Public price**, and whether it is displayed at all.
- **Number of users** included.
- **Free trial** and commitment.
- **E-invoicing** and approved platform status.

Invoicing and accounting are closely linked. Criteria specific to invoicing are covered in the guide to choosing [invoicing software]({{< relref "invoicing-software-criteria.md" >}}).

## Indy: self-managed accounting, tax filings included

Indy is online accounting software for freelancers and small companies in France. Its pricing page distinguishes four groups of legal status: sole proprietorship, auto-entrepreneur, SCI and LMNP, and companies (SAS, SASU, SARL, EURL). Prices vary with the status selected. Plan details are published on [Indy's pricing page](https://www.indy.fr/prix/).

For companies, the **Autonomie** plan is what sets it apart in this comparison. According to the check made on 27 October 2026, it costs €49 excl. VAT per month on an annual commitment (€588 excl. VAT per year), or €59 excl. VAT per month without an annual commitment. It advertises automated filings (balance sheet, income statement, VAT, CFE) and their electronic submission.

Indy also states approved platform status on its pricing page. This status is confirmed by the official DGFiP list consulted the same day.

### Indy's key features

- **Essentiel plan at €0**: e-invoicing, unlimited quotes and invoices, automatic transaction categorisation, business account with IBAN and card.
- **Autonomie plan at €49 excl. VAT per month** on an annual commitment (companies): automated balance sheet, income statement, VAT and CFE, general meeting minutes and filing of accounts, director's payslip, tax guarantee.
- **Sérénité plan from €99 excl. VAT per month** on an annual commitment (companies): a partner chartered accountant handles the filings and certifies the balance sheet.
- **Exclusivité plan from €149 excl. VAT per month** on an annual commitment (companies): a dedicated chartered accountant.
- **Monthly subscription with no commitment**, according to the FAQ on the pricing page.

Including the director's payslip in the Autonomie plan may interest presidents of SAS and SASU companies. For businesses with employees, dedicated [payroll software]({{< relref "payroll-software-key-features.md" >}}) is still needed.

## Detailed comparison: Pennylane, Sage 50 and Tiime against Indy

### Pennylane, a financial management platform

Pennylane presents its plans for freelancers (EI, SASU, EURL, SCI) with public prices: Starter at €7 excl. VAT per month, Basique at €14, Essentiel at €24 and Premium at €79. Only the Premium plan mentions an "accounting module". These plans include a single user.

For small and medium businesses, the pricing page checked on 27 October 2026 shows no prices. It lists user volumes and a custom offer for medium-sized and mid-cap companies. Pennylane also states that it is not a chartered accounting firm.

Strengths noted: a 15-day free trial with no commitment, and e-invoicing included in all plans at no extra cost according to the FAQ.

### Sage 50, classic accounting software in a cloud version

Sage 50 offers three packs, all advertised for up to 3 users and compatible with e-invoicing: Accounting from €21 excl. VAT per month, Quotes and Invoicing from €20 excl. VAT per month, and Accounting plus Invoicing from €38 excl. VAT per month.

The free trial lasts 30 days, without a credit card. Sage states that e-invoicing compliance, including connection to its approved platform, is built into the subscription.

Sage 50 suits a small team that keeps its accounts in-house. The page checked does not detail tax return production.

### Tiime, free invoicing and a link with the chartered accountant

Tiime displays four plans: Free (no charge), Initial at €9.99 excl. VAT per month, Smart from €17.99 excl. VAT per month and Business from €24.99 excl. VAT per month. Smart and Business prices rise with the number of employees, up to a quote beyond 20 employees.

Tiime highlights free e-invoicing and collaboration with the chartered accountant. Its page does not list the balance sheet or tax return among its own features: VAT figures are presented as provided "by the firm".

Paid plans come with a 60-day trial. The Initial plan requires opening a Tiime business account.

## Who is it for? Recommendations by profile

### Freelancer or SASU director who wants to handle everything alone

For this profile, Indy is the most consistent choice among the four tools compared. The Autonomie plan covers a company's annual filings, and the Sérénité plan makes it possible to switch later to a partner chartered accountant without changing tools.

### Small business with a person dedicated to accounting

Sage 50 fits this case well: classic accounting software, several users, a low entry price and a 30-day trial. Businesses that invoice a lot can choose the pack that combines accounting and invoicing.

### Business already supported by a chartered accountant

Tiime and Pennylane suit companies whose books are kept by a firm. Tiime stands out with free invoicing, Pennylane with its spending and cash flow management features. It is worth asking first which tool the firm uses.

## How to choose your accounting software

1. **Decide who keeps the books**: the owner, an employee or a firm.
2. **Check the legal status covered**: Indy's and Pennylane's plans vary by status.
3. **Check approved platform status** on the official DGFiP list, not only on the vendor's website.
4. **Compare the real annual price**, taking into account the gap between annual and monthly commitment.
5. **Test during the trial period** with real data.

Accounting software centralises sensitive data: bank statements, invoices, salaries. Access must be protected by following [cybersecurity best practices]({{< relref "small-business-cybersecurity-best-practices.md" >}}), starting with two-factor authentication. Bank synchronisation and automatic categorisation are also part of a broader effort to [automate repetitive tasks]({{< relref "automate-repetitive-tasks.md" >}}).

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Choosing on the entry price alone, when full accounting is often in a higher plan.
2. Forgetting the gap between the monthly price and the annual commitment price.
3. Confusing invoicing software with accounting software: a free plan rarely covers the balance sheet.
4. Relying on a badge on the vendor's website without checking the official list of approved platforms.
5. Switching tools without checking how to retrieve the accounting history.
