## Why the choice of invoicing software matters in France in 2026

Choosing the **best invoicing software in France** is no longer just about producing neat quotes. According to economie.gouv.fr (page consulted on 27 October 2026), every business must be able to receive e-invoices since 1 September 2026, and SMEs and micro-businesses will have to issue them from 1 September 2027.

Invoicing software must therefore rely on a platform approved by the French State. The role of these operators is detailed in the article [E-invoicing in France: what is an approved platform?]({{< relref "approved-platform-e-invoicing-france.md" >}}).

The other criteria remain the same: mandatory mentions, payment tracking, reminders, accounting export. They are covered in the guide to choosing [invoicing software]({{< relref "invoicing-software-criteria.md" >}}).

### The criteria used for the comparison

- **The free plan** and its limits.
- **The price of paid plans** and what they unlock.
- **The link with an approved platform**: the vendor itself or a partner platform.
- **VAT invoicing**, essential for a VAT-registered company.
- **Payment, reminder and purchase management**.

## Abby: free invoicing software that is also an approved platform

Abby is invoicing and management software designed for freelancers and small businesses, claiming more than 150,000 freelance users on its pricing page. It appears, under the name ABBY, on the [DGFiP approved platform list](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/je-consulte-la-liste-des-plateformes-agreees) (in French, version updated on 16 November 2026). Plan details are on [Abby's pricing page](https://abby.fr/tarifs/) (in French).

Abby shows four plans, at the same price for micro-businesses and companies:

| Abby plan | Price checked | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Basique | €0 excl. VAT per month | E-invoicing, unlimited quotes and invoices, sales and purchase ledger |
| Start | €9 excl. VAT per month | Card payments, deposit invoices, sending invoices by email |
| Plus | €15 excl. VAT per month | Business account, VAT invoicing, overdue reminders, e-signature, bank connection |
| Premium | €33 excl. VAT per month | Multiple users and accountant access, stock management, foreign currencies and languages |

### Abby's key features

- **E-invoicing from the free plan**, from a vendor that is itself on the approved platform list.
- **No commitment**: the page states cancellation at any time, with annual billing available (up to 20% off announced).
- **14 days free** to test paid plans.
- **Features for micro-entrepreneurs**: estimated Urssaf contributions, VAT threshold alerts, Urssaf declaration from Start.
- **Point to watch**: a company that charges VAT needs the Plus plan, at €15 excl. VAT per month.

For a freelancer or small business that wants to start without spending and stay compliant with the reform, Abby offers the most gradual path of the three.

## Comparative analysis: Evoliz and Henrri against Abby

### Evoliz, full management with an approved platform included

Evoliz is invoicing and management software claiming more than 47,000 users and operating since 2010, backed by the Visma group. Its pricing page, checked on 16 November 2026, shows two plans: Formule Essentielle at €29 excl. VAT per month and Formule Avancée at €49 excl. VAT per month, with a 15-day free trial.

E-invoicing goes through the Chaintrust by Visma approved platform, which appears on the DGFiP list. Evoliz also announces a double NF 203 and NF 525 certification under the French anti-VAT-fraud law.

Formule Essentielle already covers unlimited invoices and quotes, deposit and progress invoices, e-signature, AI processing of purchase documents, supplier payments, bank synchronisation and accounting exports. Formule Avancée adds recurring invoices, automatic reminders, purchase orders and delivery notes, and stock management. Each extra user costs €9 per month.

### Henrri, the broadest free invoicing

Henrri presents itself as free invoicing software "for everyone for 10 years" and claims 246,000 user businesses. Its Pro plan, at €0 excl. VAT, includes unlimited sending and receiving of e-invoices, quotes, payment tracking, overdue reminders and multi-user access.

The paid plans, shown with annual billing, cost €17 excl. VAT per month for Expert (€204 per year) and €25 excl. VAT per month for Smart (€300 per year). They add document customisation, online signature and bank synchronisation in particular.

Henrri states that it complies with e-invoicing "via a partner Approved Platform", without naming it on its pricing page. Henrri does not appear under its own name on the DGFiP list consulted. Its home page announces servers hosted in France.

## Who is each tool for? Recommendations by profile

### Micro-entrepreneur getting started

Abby is the most consistent choice: the free plan covers e-invoicing, the sales ledger and contribution estimates, and the vendor is itself an approved platform.

### Small VAT-registered company

Abby Plus, at €15 excl. VAT per month, combines VAT invoicing, reminders, e-signature and a business account. Henrri remains a free option, provided VAT handling is checked during testing.

### Business that wants to centralise sales and purchases

Evoliz suits businesses that also want to process supplier invoices, pay suppliers and track cash flow, while preparing the accountant's work.

## How to choose invoicing software in 5 steps

The criteria common to all [SaaS software]({{< relref "what-is-saas-software.md" >}}), such as subscription billing or data portability, are covered in a separate guide; the steps below focus on invoicing.

1. **Check the link with an approved platform** on the official DGFiP list.
2. **Estimate the volume** of quotes and invoices, and the number of users.
3. **Check VAT handling** if the business is VAT-registered.
4. **Plan the export to accounting** and the accountant's access.
5. **Test during the trial or free plan** with real invoices.

Invoicing software feeds the accounts: the comparison of the [best accounting software in France]({{< relref "best-accounting-software-france.md" >}}) presents tools that combine both. To also track leads and quotes, the comparison of the [best CRM for a small business]({{< relref "best-crm-small-business.md" >}}) covers CRMs that include invoicing.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Choosing a tool without checking its link with an approved platform.
2. Forgetting that VAT invoicing is not always included in the entry plan.
3. Comparing monthly prices with annually billed prices.
4. Overlooking accounting export and accountant access.
5. Staying on a free tool without checking what happens as the business grows.
