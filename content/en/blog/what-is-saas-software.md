> **In short:**
> 1. **SaaS software** (Software as a Service) is used online, with no installation: the vendor hosts, updates and secures it, and the business pays a subscription.
> 2. Vendors use several pricing models: per user, per module, by volume, or a limited free plan. The real cost also depends on commitment and first-year promotions.
> 3. SaaS differs from PaaS (a platform to build on) and IaaS (servers on demand), two other levels of cloud services.
> 4. To choose well, compare the full annual cost, data location, security, integrations and reversibility, then test during the free trial.

## Definition: what is SaaS software?

**SaaS software**, short for Software as a Service, is software a business uses online, from a web browser or an app, without installing it on its own servers. The software runs on the vendor's infrastructure.

The vendor takes care of hosting, updates, maintenance and part of the security. The business accesses the service through user accounts and pays a subscription.

Most of the tools compared on Club Digital are SaaS software: team chat, CRM, invoicing, payroll, project management and HR software.

### The characteristics of SaaS software

- **Online access**, from any connected device.
- **No installation** and no server to manage on the business side.
- **Automatic updates**, rolled out by the vendor to all customers.
- **Subscription**, monthly or annual, instead of a one-off licence.
- **Data hosted by the vendor**, or by a host it has chosen.

## SaaS or installed software: what are the differences?

| Criterion | SaaS software | Installed software |
|---|---|---|
| Where it runs | On the vendor's servers | On the company's computers or servers |
| Access | Browser or app, anywhere with internet | From the machines where it is installed |
| Updates | Automatic, handled by the vendor | Installed by the company |
| Payment | Subscription, often per user | Licence purchase, sometimes with annual maintenance |
| Server backups and security | Handled by the vendor | The company's responsibility |
| Customisation | Limited to the settings provided | Often more extensive |
| Dependency | Depends on the vendor and the connection | Depends on the company's hardware |

Some vendors offer both. Microsoft 365 is an example: web versions of the apps are available from Business Basic, and desktop versions from Business Standard. Details are in the comparison [Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace]({{< relref "microsoft-365-vs-google-workspace.md" >}}).

## SaaS, PaaS and IaaS: the three levels of cloud

SaaS is the most "finished" level of cloud services. Two other levels exist, mainly used by technical teams.

| Level | What is provided | For whom |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS (Software as a Service) | Ready-to-use software | Every team in the business |
| PaaS (Platform as a Service) | A platform to build and run applications | Developers |
| IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service) | Servers, storage and networking on demand | Technical teams and hosts |

Website hosting is closer to infrastructure. The different options are presented in the article [shared, VPS, dedicated or cloud hosting]({{< relref "shared-hosting-vps-cloud.md" >}}).

## How is SaaS software billed?

SaaS vendors use several pricing models. The examples below come from the prices published in Club Digital's comparisons, at the date they were checked.

| Pricing model | Principle | Example checked |
|---|---|---|
| Per user | A fixed price per person per month | Slack Pro: €8.25 per user per month billed monthly (9 November 2026) |
| Per user, cheaper annually | A discount for a one-year commitment | Asana Starter: €10.99 annually, €13.49 monthly (16 November 2026) |
| Per module | Each feature is paid separately | Lucca: from €0.61 to €5.81 excl. VAT per employee or claimant depending on the module (23 November 2026) |
| Subscription plus price per employee | A base fee and a price per person | PayFit: €49 excl. VAT per month for 1 to 3 employees, plus €20 per employee on the Paie plan (11 November 2026) |
| By volume | The price follows usage, for example the number of emails | Brevo: plans based on the volume of emails sent (9 November 2026) |
| Limited free plan (freemium) | A free plan, then paid plans | HubSpot CRM free for up to 2 users and 1,000 contacts (28 October 2026) |

These prices change: the vendors' pages prevail. Above all, they show that the same budget is calculated differently from one tool to another.

### SaaS pricing pitfalls

- **First-year prices**: 20% or 50% discounts that disappear on renewal.
- **Introductory prices** limited to a number of users or a period.
- **Paid add-ons**: e-signature, extra storage, priority support.
- **VAT**: prices are rarely shown in the same way (with or without VAT) from one vendor to another.
- **Annual commitment** that prevents reducing the number of users mid-year.

## Benefits and limits of SaaS software for a small business

| Benefits | Limits |
|---|---|
| Quick start, with no server or installation | Dependency on the vendor and its pricing policy |
| Updates and maintenance included | Recurring costs that add up with the number of tools |
| Access from any device, easier remote work | Operation tied to the internet connection |
| Predictable cost per user | Data hosted outside the company |
| Frequent free trials | Customisation limited to the settings provided |

For a small business without an IT department, the benefits often outweigh the limits: the vendor handles part of the technical work that would otherwise fall on the business. The main point to watch remains the growing number of subscriptions and accounts.

## How to choose SaaS software: the criteria to check

1. **Start from the team's real needs**, not the feature list.
2. **Calculate the full annual cost**: users, modules, add-ons, price after the first year.
3. **Check where data is located** and the vendor's commitments on personal data protection.
4. **Check access security**: two-factor authentication, permission management, single sign-on.
5. **List integrations** with the tools already in place.
6. **Check reversibility**: the ability to export your data in a usable format if you leave.
7. **Test during the free trial** with real use cases.

Account security is the weak point of a SaaS environment: a reused password can open several tools.
