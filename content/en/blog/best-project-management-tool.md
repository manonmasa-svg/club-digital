> **In short:**
> 1. Four project management tools are compared using their pricing pages checked on 16 November 2026: Asana, Trello, monday.com and Notion.
> 2. Asana Starter costs €10.99 per user per month billed annually (€13.49 monthly) and includes timeline and Gantt views, unlimited automation rules and unlimited free guests from that tier.
> 3. Trello has the most generous free plan (up to 10 collaborators) and the lowest paid price: $5 per user per month billed annually for Standard.
> 4. monday.com starts at €9 excl. tax (Basic) and Notion at €9.50 (Plus), per user per month billed annually. Asana's and monday.com's free plans are limited to 2 users.

## Why choose a project management tool

Choosing the **best project management tool** means choosing how the team breaks work into tasks, assigns owners and tracks deadlines. A poor choice quickly leads to forgotten tasks or a tool abandoned after a few weeks.

Project management is one of the families of tools presented in the article [Collaboration tools: the categories to know]({{< relref "team-collaboration-tools.md" >}}). It complements team chat and document sharing.

The four tools compared share the same basics: tasks, owners, deadlines, list or board views. They differ in price, free plan limits and planning views.

### The criteria used for the comparison

- **The free plan** and its real limits.
- **The price per user**, billed annually and monthly.
- **Planning views**: timeline, Gantt, calendar.
- **Included automations**.
- **Guest access** for clients or contractors.

## Asana: the most complete project management tool from the entry plan

Asana is a work management tool designed to track team projects. Its pricing page, checked on 16 November 2026, shows a free plan and three paid plans. Details are on [Asana's pricing page](https://asana.com/pricing).

| Asana plan | Annual | Monthly | What it brings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal | €0 | €0 | Up to 2 users, unlimited tasks and projects, list, board and calendar views |
| Starter | €10.99 | €13.49 | Timeline and Gantt views, dashboards, unlimited automation rules, forms, unlimited free guests |
| Advanced | €24.99 | €30.49 | Goal and team capacity planning |
| Enterprise | On request | On request | Advanced security and governance controls |

### Asana's key features

- **Planning from Starter**: timeline views and interactive Gantt charts, with task dependencies.
- **Unlimited automation rules** from Starter, to assign tasks, send alerts or update statuses.
- **Unlimited free guests**: clients and contractors collaborate without extra licences.
- **Intake forms** that create tasks automatically.
- **More than 100 integrations** from the free plan, including Slack, Google Drive and Zoom.
- **AI included** in paid plans, with a limited number of requests per user.

For a small business managing several projects with deadlines and dependencies, Asana Starter brings together in one plan what other tools often reserve for higher tiers.

## Comparative analysis: Trello, monday.com and Notion against Asana

### Trello, the simple and affordable Kanban board

Trello organises work into boards, lists and cards. According to its pricing page, the free plan accepts up to 10 collaborators and 10 boards per workspace, with unlimited cards and two-factor authentication.

Paid plans are shown in US dollars: Standard at $5 per user per month billed annually ($6 monthly), Premium at $10 ($12.50 monthly) and Enterprise at $17.50 billed annually. Standard unlocks unlimited boards and custom fields. Calendar, timeline, table and dashboard views come with Premium.

Trello remains the easiest tool for a small team to adopt. It is less suited to projects with many dependencies before the Premium plan.

### monday.com, the customisable visual platform

monday.com offers highly customisable boards. Its pricing page, checked for a team of 10 users billed annually, shows prices excluding taxes: Basic at €9, Standard at €12 and Pro at €19 per user per month. Standard and Pro had a special offer at the time of the check, compared with €14 and €22 normally.

The free plan is limited to 2 users and 3 boards. Calendar and Gantt views, guest access and 250 automation actions per month come with Standard. Pro rises to 25,000 actions per month and adds time tracking.

### Notion, the all-in-one workspace

Notion combines documents, wikis, databases and projects. Its pricing page shows a free plan, Plus at €9.50 per seat per month billed annually (€11.50 monthly) and Business at €19.50 (€23.50 monthly).

Notion's databases handle sub-tasks, dependencies and custom properties from the free plan. Page and block usage is however limited from 2 members. Business adds the Notion AI agent, SAML single sign-on and private teamspaces.

Notion suits teams that want to document and plan in one place, accepting that they will build their own tracking system.

### Summary of the differences

| Need | Best-placed tool according to the checks | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Plan with Gantt and dependencies without upgrading | Asana | Timeline and Gantt from Starter, €10.99 billed annually |
| Start for free with a small team | Trello | Free for up to 10 collaborators |
| Pay the least per user | Trello Standard | $5 per user per month billed annually |
| Work with clients or contractors | Asana | Unlimited free guests from Starter |
| Combine documentation and projects | Notion | Documents, wikis and databases in one tool |
| Heavily customise boards | monday.com | Configurable boards and columns, Gantt from Standard |

## Who is each tool for? Recommendations by profile

### Small business managing several projects at once

Asana Starter is the most consistent choice: timeline and Gantt views, unlimited automations and dashboards are included from the entry plan, without moving up a tier.

### Small team that wants a simple tool

Trello lets a team start for free with up to 10 collaborators, with a card-based approach everyone understands quickly. Moving to Standard remains inexpensive.

### Team that wants to centralise everything

Notion suits teams that want to write procedures, meeting notes and projects in the same place. monday.com will appeal to teams that want highly visual, customised boards.

## How to choose a project management tool in 5 steps

1. **List the projects and their dependencies**: simple task tracking or real planning?
2. **Count users and guests**: free plan limits change everything.
3. **Calculate the real annual cost**, comparing annual and monthly billing.
4. **Check integrations** with the team's chat and other tools.
5. **Test with a real project** during the free plan or trial.

Project management often relies on a team chat app: the comparison [Slack, Microsoft Teams or Google Chat]({{< relref "slack-vs-teams-vs-google-chat.md" >}}) helps choose one that connects to it. To link the project tool with other software, the comparison [Zapier vs Make vs n8n]({{< relref "zapier-vs-make-vs-n8n.md" >}}) covers automation tools.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Choosing the richest tool when the team needs a simple task board.
2. Forgetting the user limits of free plans.
3. Comparing prices in dollars and euros without saying so.
4. Not naming someone responsible for keeping the tool alive.
5. Relying on a special price without knowing the usual rate.
