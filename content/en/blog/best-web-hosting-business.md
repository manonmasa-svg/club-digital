## Why the choice of host matters for a business website

Choosing the **best web host** for a business website means choosing where the website, its databases and often its email addresses run. A poor choice costs slow pages, outages or a higher bill after a year.

The four hosts compared all offer shared hosting, the option suited to most brochure websites and business blogs. The differences between shared hosting, VPS, dedicated servers and cloud are covered in the article [Web hosting: shared, VPS, dedicated or cloud?]({{< relref "shared-hosting-vps-cloud.md" >}}).

### The criteria used for the comparison

- **The entry price and the renewal price**, which are often very different.
- **The commitment**: period paid in advance and cancellation terms.
- **Resources**: number of websites, storage, mailboxes.
- **The stated server location**, useful for a GDPR assessment.
- **The ability to test** or get a refund.

## First-year price and renewal price: the gap to watch

The most common trap in web hosting is the first-year discount. The headline prices only apply at the start of the contract.

| OVHcloud Perso | €2.99 excl. VAT per month | €5.99 excl. VAT per month |
| Hostinger Premium | €2.49 per month (48 months paid in advance) | €9.99 per month |

Over several years, the ranking can reverse: a plan with a very low headline price can cost more than a stable plan from the second year onwards. The useful calculation is the total cost over three years, renewals included.

## o2switch: unlimited hosting in French data centres

o2switch is a French host that states, on its offer page, that it owns its data centres in France, in Clermont-Ferrand. All its plans include unlimited websites, databases, mailboxes, NVMe disk space and monthly traffic. Plans are detailed on [o2switch's hosting page](https://www.o2switch.fr/hebergement-illimite/).

According to the check made on 29 October 2026, the range has three plans, billed annually. Grow costs €7 excl. VAT per month, or €84 excl. VAT per year, with no first-year discount. Cloud and Pro are shown with a first-year discount (€1.86 and €6.25 excl. VAT per month), then renewed at the usual rate, which the page shows struck through at €16 and €24 excl. VAT per month.

### o2switch's key features

- **Unlimited**: websites, databases, mailboxes, NVMe disk space and traffic, on all plans.
- **Guaranteed resources**: from 8 vCPU and 16 GB of memory (Grow) to 24 vCPU and 64 GB (Pro).
- **Daily backups** kept for 30 days (Grow), 45 days (Cloud) or 90 days (Pro).
- **Domain name** free for the first year with Grow, permanently included with Cloud and Pro.
- **30-day money-back guarantee** on all plans.
- **Prices including VAT** not shown: the page states that they depend on the country and billing conditions.

## Detailed comparison: OVHcloud, Hostinger and Infomaniak against o2switch

### OVHcloud, the lowest entry price

OVHcloud offers an Eco range with a free plan (1 website, 100 MB, 1 email address) included with each domain name purchased. The Starter plan costs €1.59 excl. VAT per month in the first year, then €2.59 excl. VAT, for 1 website, 1 GB of storage and 2 email addresses. The Perso plan costs €2.99 excl. VAT per month in the first year, then €5.99 excl. VAT, for 15 websites, 100 GB and 10 email addresses.

Plans include an SSL certificate, anti-DDoS protection, automatic backups and unlimited traffic. The offer page consulted does not specify where web hosting servers are located.

### Hostinger, the headline price with a long commitment

Hostinger shows three plans: Premium at €2.49, Unlimited at €3.49 and Cloud Startup at €7.99 per month. These prices require a 48-month commitment paid in advance, or €119.52 at sign-up for Premium. On renewal, prices rise to €9.99, €16.99 and €23.99 per month.

The Premium plan includes 3 websites and 20 GB of SSD storage, the Unlimited plan unlimited websites and 50 GB NVMe. The page states that the plan can be cancelled at any time, with a full refund within 30 days. It lets you choose the server location, without specifying countries.

### Infomaniak, hosting in Switzerland

Infomaniak states that it develops and hosts its infrastructure in Switzerland. Its Web Hosting plan starts at €5.75 excl. VAT per month, with 250 GB of SSD space, 20 websites, unlimited databases and traffic, and one free email address. The Web + Mail plan starts at €7.25 excl. VAT per month with 5 email addresses.

Infomaniak offers a 30-day free trial (a credit card is required) and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Support is advertised by phone and email, 7 days a week.

## Who is it for? Recommendations by profile

### Small business with a brochure website and several email addresses

o2switch is the most consistent choice among the four: a single subscription covers all the company's websites and mailboxes, with a price that does not rise on renewal for the Grow plan and servers stated to be in France.

### Freelancer with a single small website

OVHcloud's Starter plan is enough for a light website, provided that 1 GB of storage and 2 email addresses cover the needs.

### Business looking for an independent European host

Infomaniak suits organisations that prefer an independent European host. Switzerland is not a member of the European Union: the GDPR assessment must take this into account.

## How to choose your web host

1. **Count the websites and mailboxes** needed today and in two years.
2. **Calculate the cost over three years**, renewals included.
3. **Check the period paid in advance** and the cancellation terms.
4. **Check the stated location** of servers and backups.
5. **Test during the refund period** with the real website.

Shared hosting is enough for a brochure website. To run a self-hosted tool, such as the Community edition covered in the comparison [Zapier, Make or n8n]({{< relref "zapier-vs-make-vs-n8n.md" >}}), a VPS is often needed. In all cases, [cybersecurity best practices]({{< relref "small-business-cybersecurity-best-practices.md" >}}) apply to the host's customer area: two-factor authentication and unique passwords.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Choosing on the first-year price without checking the renewal price.
2. Paying four years in advance without having tested the service.
3. Leaving mailboxes out of the needs calculation.
4. Not checking where backups are stored.
5. Linking the domain name and hosting without knowing how to separate them when leaving.
