## Why use expense management software

**Expense management software** replaces spreadsheets, stapled paper receipts and email reminders. The employee photographs the receipt, the software extracts the amounts, the manager approves and accounting receives entries ready to import.

For a small business, the gain covers three points: less data entry, fewer lost receipts and faster reimbursements. Most of the tools compared also calculate mileage allowances.

Expense reports then feed the accounts: the tools that receive them are presented in the comparison of the [best accounting software in France]({{< relref "best-accounting-software-france.md" >}}).

### The ranking criteria

- **Price transparency** and level for a small team.
- **Free plan or trial.**
- **Receipt scanning** and mileage calculation.
- **Approval workflows** and accounting exports.
- **Payment cards**, useful to avoid employees advancing expenses.

## 1. Rydoo: the expense specialist with a public price

Rydoo is software dedicated to managing expense reports and supplier invoices. Its pricing page, checked on 24 November 2026, shows two self-service plans, from 5 users. Details are on [Rydoo's pricing page](https://www.rydoo.com/fr/tarifs/) (in French).

| Rydoo plan | Annual | Monthly | What it includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials | €8 per user per month | €10 | Mobile app with receipt scanning, unlimited expenses, mileage allowances, single approval workflow, certified paperless expense reports |
| Pro | €10 | €12 | Per diems, online accounting integrations, automatic transaction matching, multi-level approval |
| Business | Tailored | | From 30 active users: Rydoo cards, API access, priority support |
| Enterprise | Tailored | | From 50 active users: SSO, advanced dashboards, HR connection |

Rydoo states that on Business and Enterprise plans, only people who actively use the software are billed. For a small business that wants a specialised, ready-to-use tool with a price known in advance, Rydoo Essentials is the clearest entry point in the comparison.

## 2. Expensify: the cheapest, with a free plan

Expensify shows its prices in euros: the Submit plan is free to scan receipts, track mileage and submit expense reports. The Collect plan costs €5 per member per month and adds reimbursements, corporate card management, approvals and QuickBooks and Xero integrations. The Control plan starts at €8 per member per month, tailored, for complex approval workflows. A 30-day free trial is offered.

## 3. Lucca Business expenses: expense reports within the HRIS

Lucca offers a Business expenses module at €5.81 excl. VAT per claimant per month, according to its pricing page checked on 23 November 2026 (indicative price for companies with more than 100 employees). Lucca states that it has no minimum commitment. The module integrates with its other HR solutions, compared in the article on the [best HR software for a small business]({{< relref "best-hr-software-small-business.md" >}}).

## 4. Spendesk: cards and expenses in one platform

Spendesk shows tailored pricing. Its "Foundations" package includes unlimited physical cards, single-use and recurring cards, OCR receipt data extraction, expense reimbursement, budget tracking and integrations with Xero, QuickBooks or Datev. Add-on modules cover procurement and supplier invoices.

## 5. Mooncard: the configurable card

Mooncard offers two plans on quotation, Original and Hub. Original includes 5 seats, one physical or virtual card per user, the spend management software, OCR for receipts and an accounting engine. The pricing page shows a €10 issuing fee per card and 0% top-up fees.

## 6. Medius Expense (formerly Expensya): the Premium offer on quotation

Expensya is becoming Medius Expense, according to a banner on its website. Its Premium offer, on request, includes unlimited OCR, legal archiving, mileage allowances, per diems, management of several subsidiaries and multi-level approval workflows. Card payments and bank integration are sold as add-ons.

## 7. Payhawk: expenses, travel and invoices

Payhawk combines modules on quotation: Cards & Expenses, Travel and Accounts Payable. A "Growth Program" at €149 per month for 24 months targets single-entity companies established in the UK or the European Economic Area with fewer than 10 employees, with a 7-day free trial for eligible customers.

## 8. Jenji: free for freelancers

Jenji offers Jenji Solo, free and aimed at freelancers, and a small business plan billed per user, whose price is not shown. Announced features include OCR, automatic mileage calculation, offline mode and a digital vault with archiving of evidential value.

## 9. PayFit: expenses reimbursed through payroll

PayFit offers expense reports as an add-on to its payroll service, at €3 per employee per month, according to the page checked on 11 November 2026. The add-on highlights mid-month reimbursement and spending limit policies. It only makes sense for businesses already using PayFit, presented in the comparison of the [best payroll software in France]({{< relref "best-payroll-software-france.md" >}}).

## 10. Qonto: expenses and a business account

Qonto is an online business account. Its pricing page, checked on 24 November 2026, shows for freelancers a Basic plan at €9 excl. VAT per month billed annually (€108 per year) and Smart at €19 excl. VAT. The feature table mentions centralisation of expense reports and reimbursement of personal expenses, the latter not being included in every plan. A one-month free trial is offered.

## How to choose expense management software in 5 steps

1. **Count the employees who advance expenses**: this is often the pricing basis.
2. **Decide whether cards are useful** to remove advances.
3. **Check exports** to the accounting or invoicing software.
4. **Test receipt scanning** on real receipts during the trial.
5. **Compare the annual cost**, including card fees and add-ons.

Most of these tools are online software billed by subscription: how they work and their pricing models are explained in the guide [What is SaaS software?]({{< relref "what-is-saas-software.md" >}}).

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Comparing a per-user price with a per-claimant price without saying so.
2. Forgetting card issuing fees and add-on modules.
3. Choosing a tool with no export to your accounting software.
4. Neglecting receipt archiving.
5. Imposing the tool without training employees to scan receipts.
