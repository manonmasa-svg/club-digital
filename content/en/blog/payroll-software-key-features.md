> **In short:**
> 1. Payroll software produces payslips, including absences and variable pay.
> 2. In France, it also prepares the DSN, the monthly social security filing.
> 3. How the vendor updates calculation rules is a decisive criterion.
> 4. Payroll data is sensitive and must be protected.

## What payroll software is for

**Payroll software** calculates pay, social contributions and net amounts, then issues payslips. It keeps each employee's history and prepares social security filings. For a business with employees, it reduces calculation errors and the time spent each month.

Payroll is one of the most regulated processes in a business. Contribution rates, ceilings and the rules of each collective agreement change regularly. A spreadsheet quickly reaches its limits with this complexity, and an error on a payslip has direct consequences for both the employee and the employer.

## The payroll cycle, step by step

Each month, payroll production follows roughly the same sequence:

1. **Collect variables**: overtime, bonuses, absences, new starters and leavers.
2. **Calculate**: the software applies contribution rules and produces the payslips.
3. **Check**: review differences from the previous month and special cases.
4. **Approve and distribute**: issue payslips and make them available to employees.
5. **File**: generate and submit the DSN.
6. **Post to accounting**: export payroll entries to the accounting system.

Good software supports each of these steps, with alerts for anomalies and a history of changes.

## Essential features

- **Payslip production** with variable pay (overtime, bonuses).
- **Absence management**: leave, sick leave, other absences.
- **Social security filings**: generating the DSN.
- **Accounting export** to the accounting software, or to the [invoicing software]({{< relref "invoicing-software-criteria.md" >}}) when it includes an accounting module.
- **Employee portal** to view payslips online.

Other features are often useful day to day: salary simulation before hiring, expense report management, leave balance tracking, or end-of-contract documents.

## Electronic payslips

According to [Service Public Entreprendre](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F559) (page in French, consulted on 27 October 2026), electronic payslips have been the rule since 2017 and paper the exception, unless the employee objects. The employer must tell the employee how to access their payslips. The employer also keeps a copy of each payslip, on paper or electronically, for 5 years.

For payroll software, this translates into two concrete needs: a secure space where employees find their payslips, and reliable archiving on the employer's side. It is worth asking the vendor how long documents remain accessible and how they can be retrieved when changing tools.

## Points to check before choosing

| Criterion | Question to ask the vendor |
|---|---|
| Regulatory updates | How are changes to calculation rules built in? |
| Collective agreement | Is the company's agreement supported? |
| Integrations | Does the software connect to the HRIS and accounting? |
| Support | What help is available for the initial setup? |

The initial configuration deserves particular care. It determines the accuracy of every payslip that follows. Many businesses have this setup, or the review of the first months, carried out by a payroll professional before producing payslips on their own.

## Payroll software and HRIS: what is the difference

Payroll software focuses on calculating pay and filings. An HRIS, or human resources information system, covers a wider scope: employee records, annual reviews, training, recruitment, time management. Some vendors offer both in a single suite, others specialise and connect to each other. For a small business, starting with payroll and adding HR modules later is a common approach.

## Sensitive data and teamwork

Payroll data (salaries, bank details, absences) is among the most sensitive in a business. Exchanges between HR, accounting and management are better handled through secure [collaboration tools]({{< relref "team-collaboration-tools.md" >}}) than through email attachments. Payroll software can also connect to the [CRM]({{< relref "what-is-a-crm.md" >}}) or other tools to track sales commissions.

A few habits limit the risks: restrict access to the payroll module to the people concerned, enable two-factor authentication and check the vendor's backup policy. These measures are part of the [cybersecurity best practices]({{< relref "small-business-cybersecurity-best-practices.md" >}}) that apply to the whole business.
