## Why a small business needs business antivirus

Choosing the **best antivirus for a small business** means protecting devices that hold client files, bank access and business email. An infected computer can halt all activity, especially in a ransomware attack.

Business versions differ from consumer antivirus in that they cover several devices under one licence and often add simplified management for the person in charge.

Antivirus is only one layer of security. It complements the measures covered in [small business cybersecurity best practices]({{< relref "small-business-cybersecurity-best-practices.md" >}}), such as backups and updates.

### The criteria used for the comparison

- **The real price**, taking the unit (device, user, bundle) and taxes into account.
- **The renewal price**, often higher than the first year.
- **The devices covered**: computers, smartphones, servers.
- **Included features**: ransomware protection, VPN, advanced detection.
- **The free trial** to test before buying.

## ESET Small Business Security: turnkey protection for very small businesses

ESET presents Small Business Security as complete protection for very small businesses, from 1 to 25 devices. Details are on [ESET's purchase page](https://www.eset.com/fr/acheter/) (in French).

According to the check made on 19 November 2026, the plan for 5 devices and 1 year costs €169.99 incl. VAT, shown at €118.99 incl. VAT as part of a Black Friday promotion, or €9.92 per month according to the page. The price is stated as valid for a first subscription.

### ESET Small Business Security's key features

- **Devices covered**: PCs, macOS, smartphones, tablets and Windows servers, with features varying by system.
- **Announced protection**: scam blocking, ransomware remediation, server protection.
- **Unlimited VPN included**, useful when connecting from public networks.
- **Remote installation help** offered with the purchase.
- **30-day free trial**, no credit card required.

For a small business without an IT department, ESET brings the company's computers, phones and server together in a single bundle, with a VPN and installation help.

## Comparative analysis: Microsoft Defender for Business and Avast against ESET

### Microsoft Defender for Business, protection per user

Microsoft Defender for Business is sold on its own or within some Microsoft 365 plans. According to its page, checked on 19 November 2026, it costs €2.60 excl. VAT per user per month billed annually, with automatic renewal, or €31.20 excl. VAT per user per year.

The offer covers up to 300 users and 5 devices per user, with no minimum number of devices, on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android. Microsoft highlights AI-based endpoint protection with automatic attack disruption, threat and vulnerability management, guided onboarding and monthly summary reports.

For a business already using Microsoft 365, it is the most consistent and cheapest option per user. It does require some familiarity with Microsoft's admin consoles.

### Avast Business, pricing per device

Avast Business shows three plans, with prices excluding VAT per device per year. According to the check made on 19 November 2026:

| Avast plan | First year | Renewal |
|---|---|---|
| Essential Business Security | €28.26 excl. VAT | €35.33 excl. VAT |
| Premium Business Security | €35.90 excl. VAT | €44.88 excl. VAT |
| Ultimate Business Security | €43.54 excl. VAT | €54.43 excl. VAT |

The 20% discount applies to the first year only. Avast offers a 30-day free trial with no payment. The page notes that some features are not available on macOS.

## Who is each antivirus for? Recommendations by profile

### Very small business without IT staff

ESET Small Business Security is the simplest choice: one bundle covers computers, phones and server, with remote installation help and a 30-day trial without a credit card.

### Small business using Microsoft 365

Microsoft Defender for Business fits into the existing environment, with the lowest price per user of the three and up to 5 devices per person.

### Business that wants to pay device by device

Avast Business lets you adjust the number of devices and the level of protection, as long as you plan for the price increase on renewal.

## How to choose business antivirus in 5 steps

1. **List the devices**: computers, smartphones, tablets, servers.
2. **Compare the cost over two years**, renewal included, converting all prices to the same basis (with or without VAT).
3. **Check the management console** and who will monitor it.
4. **Test during the free trial** on a few machines before rolling out.
5. **Complement antivirus** with staff awareness and strong passwords.

Antivirus is not enough against targeted fraudulent messages: warning signs and habits are covered in the article [How can a business spot and avoid phishing?]({{< relref "spot-avoid-phishing-business.md" >}}). Passwords remain the main entry point: the comparison of the [best business password manager]({{< relref "best-business-password-manager.md" >}}) helps choose the right tool.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Comparing a price including VAT with a price excluding VAT without saying so.
2. Forgetting the price increase on renewal.
3. Protecting computers but not business smartphones.
4. Installing antivirus with no one to follow up on alerts.
5. Treating antivirus as sufficient protection on its own.
