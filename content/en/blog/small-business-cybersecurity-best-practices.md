> **In short:**
> 1. Two-factor authentication protects accounts even if a password is stolen.
> 2. Updates fix known vulnerabilities and should be applied quickly.
> 3. Regular backups, including an offline copy, make it possible to recover after an incident.
> 4. Staff awareness remains the best defence against phishing.

## Why cybersecurity also matters for small organisations

**Cybersecurity** is not just for large groups. Small businesses use the same online tools (email, [invoicing software]({{< relref "invoicing-software-criteria.md" >}}), shared storage) and face the same common attacks: phishing, password theft, ransomware.

## The basic measures

| Measure | What it protects |
|---|---|
| Two-factor authentication | Online accounts if a password is stolen |
| Password manager | Prevents reusing the same password |
| Regular updates | Devices and software against known vulnerabilities |
| Backups with an offline copy | Data in case of failure or ransomware |
| Limited admin accounts | The system if a workstation is compromised |

## Training and preparing the team

Most attacks start with a deceptive message. Learning to check the sender, to be wary of urgent requests and to report any doubt greatly reduces the risk. It also helps to know in advance who to contact in case of an incident: in France, the public platform [cybermalveillance.gouv.fr](https://www.cybermalveillance.gouv.fr/) supports businesses that fall victim to an attack.

## Securing tools and hosting too

The choice of [web hosting]({{< relref "shared-hosting-vps-cloud.md" >}}) affects the security level of the company website. Employee data handled by [payroll software]({{< relref "payroll-software-key-features.md" >}}) also deserves particular attention.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What are the cybersecurity best practices for a small business?</summary>

The basics are to enable two-factor authentication, use unique passwords managed by a password manager, apply updates, back up data regularly with an offline copy and train the team to recognise phishing.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What should you do after a cyberattack?</summary>

Disconnect the affected devices from the network, do not pay a ransom without seeking advice, keep the evidence and get support. In France, the public platform cybermalveillance.gouv.fr directs businesses to suitable providers and procedures.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is two-factor authentication important?</summary>

It adds a second check on top of the password, for example a code in an app. A stolen password is then no longer enough to access the account.

</details>
