faq:
  - question: "What is SaaS software?"
    answer: "SaaS (Software as a Service) software is used online, from a browser or an app, without being installed on the company's own server. The vendor hosts the software and handles updates and maintenance, and the customer usually pays a subscription based on the number of users or the features chosen."
  - question: "How do you choose software for your business?"
    answer: "Start from the team's real needs rather than the feature list. Then check integrations with the tools already in place, access security, data location, cancellation terms and the ability to export your data. A trial period, when the vendor offers one, allows testing on concrete cases."
  - question: "When does e-invoicing become mandatory in France?"
    answer: "According to economie.gouv.fr, consulted on 27 October 2026, all businesses must be able to receive electronic invoices since 1 September 2026, and large and mid-sized companies must also issue them. Small and micro-businesses will have to issue them from 1 September 2027."
  - question: "What is a CRM used for?"
    answer: "A CRM centralises a company's contacts, interaction history and sales opportunities. It lets sales, marketing and customer service teams share the same up-to-date information and never miss a follow-up."
  - question: "What is no-code?"
    answer: "No-code refers to tools that let you build applications or automations without writing code, through a visual interface. They are used, for example, to connect several software tools and automate repetitive tasks."
  - question: "What is the difference between automation and artificial intelligence?"
    answer: "Classic automation runs fixed rules: when an event occurs, an action is triggered. Artificial intelligence handles tasks that require interpreting content, such as sorting emails or summarising a document. The two are often combined, with human review of the AI's results."
  - question: "How can a small business protect itself against phishing?"
    answer: "The basic measures are two-factor authentication on important accounts, unique passwords managed by a password manager, raising the team's awareness of suspicious messages and checking through another channel any urgent request for payment or change of bank details."
  - question: "Is the cloud compatible with the GDPR?"
    answer: "Yes, provided you choose a provider that governs data processing by contract, states where data and backups are hosted and offers security guarantees. Hosting within the European Union makes compliance simpler when personal data is processed."
  - question: "What is an HRIS?"
    answer: "An HRIS, or human resources information system, brings together staff management tools: employee records, absences, reviews, training, recruitment and sometimes payroll. It centralises HR data and automates part of the administrative work."
