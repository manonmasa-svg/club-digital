## No cookies on Club Digital

The Club Digital website sets no cookies and uses no other tracker on visitors' devices. That is why no consent banner is displayed.

According to the French data protection authority (CNIL), which sets out the applicable rules on its page [Cookies et traceurs : que dit la loi ?](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/cookies-et-autres-traceurs/regles/cookies/que-dit-la-loi) (in French), some trackers require the user's prior consent. Club Digital uses none of them.

## What this means in practice

| Item | Situation on Club Digital |
|---|---|
| Audience measurement | No tool installed |
| Advertising | None |
| Social media buttons | None |
| Videos, maps or embedded content | None |
| Fonts | Hosted on the website itself |
| Images | Hosted on the website itself |


## Websites the links point to

A link to an external website (software vendor, public source) leads to a website that applies its own cookie rules.
