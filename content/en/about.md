## A blog about business digital tools

Club Digital breaks down the software, artificial intelligence and digital tools that shape everyday business life: invoicing, accounting, CRM, automation, cybersecurity, hosting, payroll and collaboration tools.

The site is written for small business owners, freelancers, administrative, marketing and HR teams, and more broadly for anyone who has to choose, roll out or make better use of a digital tool.

## What the site publishes

- Guides to understand a family of tools and how they are used.
- Comparisons between software and services, based on information checked on vendor websites.
- Definitions to understand acronyms and technical concepts.
- Practical tips to secure, automate and organise work.

## How the articles are written

Prices, features and commercial terms change quickly. When an article mentions this kind of information, it is checked on the vendor's website and the date of the check is stated. The full method is described on the [Editorial policy]({{< relref "editorial-policy.md" >}}) page.
